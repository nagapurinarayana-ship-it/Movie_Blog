HOW WE LABEL VERDICTS

Numbers first, verdict second.

MovieBlog distinguishes reported collections from commercial verdicts. A headline such as “hit” or “flop” is shown as a source-reported performance signal; a definitive theatrical verdict requires reliable budget, distributor-rights and recovery data.

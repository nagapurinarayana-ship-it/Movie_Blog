# MovieBlog Phase 8 — Content Inventory

Phase 8 turns the completed platform into a maintainable publishing system.

## Goal
Populate the site with useful, verified inventory without fabricated facts or bulk low-quality content.

## Workflow
Trend signal → research → source verification → draft → entity linking → editorial review → publish → index.

## Priority clusters
1. Movie entity pages
2. Box-office tracking
3. OTT availability
4. Actor/director pages
5. Original entertainment news

The repository includes contracts for the inventory, content queue and data-refresh sources. These are intentionally empty until verified data is available.

## Zero-budget rule
Use existing/free services only where their terms permit the intended use. Do not introduce paid dependencies just to fill inventory.

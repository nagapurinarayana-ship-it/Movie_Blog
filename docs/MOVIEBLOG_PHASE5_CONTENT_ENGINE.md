# MovieBlog Phase 5 — Content Engine

## Delivered
- News hub and article routes.
- Local article content model in `data/articles.json`.
- Content-opportunity workflow model in `data/content-opportunities.json`.
- `NewsArticle` JSON-LD on article pages.
- Entity-linking hooks from stories to movie, actor and director records.
- News/trending navigation and sitemap coverage.
- PWA cache coverage for content pages and scripts.

## Editorial safeguards
Trending data is a discovery signal, not a factual source. MovieBlog must verify claims before publication, write original coverage, respect third-party licenses, and avoid copying competitor articles.

## Workflow
Trend signal → research → verify → write original story → link entities → human review → publish.

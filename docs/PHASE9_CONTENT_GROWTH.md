# MovieBlog Phase 9 — Content Growth

## Goal

Turn current search and entertainment signals into a prioritized editorial queue while keeping publication factual, original and source-traceable.

## Current priority clusters

1. Awarapan 2 — release, cast, review and box-office intent.
2. Batwara 1947 — release, cast, review and box-office intent.
3. Awarapan 2 vs Batwara 1947 — advance-booking and opening-weekend comparison.
4. Spider-Man: Brand New Day — India box-office and third-weekend tracking.
5. Spider-Man vs the Independence Day releases — competition and screens.
6. Microdrama in India — explanatory trend coverage.

## Editorial rule

Trending data tells us what deserves research; it does not establish facts. Every published story must be independently verified, original, dated and connected to the relevant MovieBlog entities.

## Refresh cadence

- Re-check fast-moving box-office stories daily during theatrical runs.
- Re-check release/OTT information whenever an official source changes.
- Refresh trend opportunities frequently enough to avoid stale topics.
- Retire opportunities when the search demand or news cycle has materially passed.

## Zero-budget principle

Prefer free sources, existing provider allowances and first-party announcements. Do not add a paid data dependency just to increase content volume.
